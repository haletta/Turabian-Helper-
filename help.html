Turabian Helper

A Word add-in for Turabian 9th ed. notes-bibliography papers, following the Denver Seminary Writing Center guides.

Open it

In Word, go to the Home tab and click Turabian Helper at the right end of the ribbon.

A typical paper

  1. Title page tab: fill in the form and insert the title page.
  2. Write, using Zotero for footnotes and the bibliography. Use the Scripture tab for Bible citations.
  3. Use the Format tab's heading and block-quotation buttons as you go.
  4. Before submitting: Format → Set up this paper, then Check → Check my paper and work through the list.

Undo

Every change the add-in makes can be undone with Ctrl+Z (Windows) or ⌘Z (Mac).
